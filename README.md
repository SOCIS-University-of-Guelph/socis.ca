The official website for the **Society of Computer and Information Sciences (SOCIS)** at the **University of Guelph**.

SOCIS is the academic society representing students in the Bachelor of Computing program. We support student success through events, funding, advocacy, and by empowering the university’s leading computing clubs.

---

## Clubs Supported by SOCIS

SOCIS directly funds and supports several computing-focused clubs at UofG, including:

- **Guelph Cloud Computing Club (GCCC)**  
- **Google Developer Groups – Guelph (GDG)**  
- **Guelph Cyber Security Society (GCSS)**  
- **Guelph Women in Computer Science (GWICS)**  
- **Guelph Coding Community (GCC)**  

These clubs provide hands-on learning opportunities, workshops, competitions, and community-building events for students across computing disciplines.
